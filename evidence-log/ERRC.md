# Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create Grid

**Status:** Confirmed value innovation hypothesis requiring evidence. Updated 12 Oct 2026: Reduce move now evidence-supported at reservation level (A/B test); owners n=5; card-trial support removed after sprint feedback.

**Target user:** Owners and managers of independent boutique fitness studios in Casablanca and Rabat (about 15–25 classes a week, owner sets the schedule) with a recurring off-peak class running well below capacity. Bookers: active people with an unexpected free window and no commitment to the studio.

**Job to Be Done:** When a recurring off-peak class keeps running with a few people in it, fill those seats with new people at the normal price, choosing which seats are offered, without regulars learning to expect discounts (`jtbd.md`).

**Strongest current alternative:** Doing nothing: running the class near-empty or cutting it, after discounting has failed.

**Evidence basis for the baseline:** Real-user evidence, n=5 owners. Owner B ran a 2-person class rather than cancel; Owner E keeps a 2-of-8 Friday class running for 2 regulars; Owner A cut a class after months of low attendance; Owners A and C tried discounts and stopped; Owners D and E post on Instagram with inconsistent results (`user-feedback.md`). Limitation: five owners, stated in interviews, not observed. On the booker side, the strongest alternative is the gym membership already paid for (G1, reported; desk research shows one boutique class costs about a month of gym), recorded in `market-alternatives.md`.

## Value innovation hypothesis

Studios stop paying for empty seats with discounts (Eliminate) and stop asking newcomers to prepay a pack (Reduce), which lowers the cost and risk of a first visit for both sides. In exchange, newcomers get a confirmed place in minutes (Raise), and owners get something no current alternative offers: control over which seats go to new clients only, plus proof of whether those clients are new (Create). If it holds, off-peak classes fill with net-new people at full price, without regulars expecting deals.

This remains a hypothesis, not a validated blue ocean or confirmed business model.

## Critical outstanding validation points

1. ~~Does removing the pack raise reservations by ≥30 pp over the 3-class pack?~~ **Answered at reservation level: yes, +36 pp, p = 0.011** (`ab-test-results.md`). Still open: does it hold when people must turn up and pay?
2. Do newcomers accept full studio price (300 MAD) for one class, given that it costs about a month of gym? 16 of 25 reserved at that price, but 10 of 27 decliners cited price or their gym.
3. How much does certainty of a spot matter on top of no-pack? The test held it constant; G2 and G4 say it decides last-minute bookings.
4. Would owners release seats this way instead of using their own discovery packs, and can they do it in minutes with their current tools?
5. Do trial goers come back at full price, or come once and vanish? (Phase 2 pilot)

## Evidence boundaries

- **Real evidence:** 5 owner interviews (discount rejection, quiet slots, seat-control conditions); 4 goer interviews (G1's unused pack and gym fallback; G2's and G4's abandoned DM bookings; G5's pack avoidance and beginner nerves); the A/B reservation test with 50 class-goers (real reservation messages, fictional studio, no payment).
- **Removed (sprint feedback, 12 Oct):** the three card trials and "Example-G3" were illustrative examples, not real data. They no longer support any row of this grid.
- **Desk research:** class prices (250–400 MAD), gym membership (~314 MAD/month), studio counts, Core Studio's 3-class discovery pack, no ClassPass presence found in Morocco. This shows what exists, not what our users choose.
- **Team and AI inference:** that the Create move is genuinely new for owners in Casablanca and Rabat; the cost effects are directional, not measured. This grid was drafted with AI from the evidence log and must be checked row by row by the team (`ai-usage-log.md`).
- **Contradictions and limits:** price is still a live barrier (one class costs about a month of gym; 10 of 27 A/B decliners cited price or their gym). Arm B asked for 900 MAD up front, so "commitment" and "upfront cash" can't be separated. NOVA's experience (packs sold, subscriptions didn't) differs from our result, possibly because NOVA's credits work across many studios.
