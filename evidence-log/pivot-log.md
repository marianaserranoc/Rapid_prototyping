## 2. Five Whys — why are off-peak seats empty?

| Why? | Answer | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Why is Owner A's Tuesday 11:00 class at about 3 of 10? | Her regulars are at work at that time | Owner A (interview) |
| 2. Why doesn't she fill it with other people? | New people don't come without a low-risk reason to try | Hypothesis |
| 3. Why don't new people try? | They'd have to buy a pack first, aren't sure they'll get a spot, or find one class expensive next to their gym | G1, G2, price research |
| 4. Why don't studios just sell cheaper single classes? | It looks like a discount; regulars notice and want the same deal | Owners A, B, C |
| 5. Why is that a dead end today? | There's no way to sell a full-price single seat to newcomers only without regulars seeing it | **Root cause** |

The root cause is what the product addresses (`ERRC.md`, Create move). Steps 2 and 3 are the parts the reservation test checks.

## 3. Pivots decided in advance (reservation test)

Written before results come in, so the evidence picks the direction, not us. Full rules in `experiment-card.md`.

| If the result is… | And the decline reasons say… | We will… |
|---|---|---|
| A reserves ≥40 pp more than B | — | **Continue:** pilot with 2–3 real studios and measure attendance and return |
| Gap under 20 pp | Mostly price or "prefer my gym" | **Pivot on price:** explore a new-clients-only first-visit price with owners |
| Gap under 20 pp | Mostly "not sure I'd get a spot" | **Pivot to certainty:** instant booking for studios' existing classes |
| Gap under 20 pp | Mostly "don't know the coach" | **Pivot to trust:** coach profiles, class videos, reviews |
| Almost no one reserves in either group | — | **Change the slot** and rerun before concluding |
| Gap of 20–39 pp | — | **Rerun** with 5 more people per group |
