**Rules for this file**
- Only real people. AI-simulated responses (`gym_owners.md`, `personas/hypothetical/ai-persona-*`) and illustrative examples (`oz-trials.md`, "Example-G3") are **not listed here**.
- Stated intent ("I'd book it") is labelled as such and never counted as behaviour.
- Use initials or codes only. No names, phone numbers or screenshots of private messages.

## 3. What the feedback changed

| Decision | Before | After | Triggered by |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary test design | Compared commitment terms only | Instant confirmation held constant in both arms so certainty can't explain the result | G2 (confirmed by G4 on 12 Oct) |
| Owner offer | A trial mechanism for any goer | Seats chosen by the owner, offered to **new clients only**, with new-vs-regular tracking | Owners B, C (confirmed by D and E) |
| JTBD trigger | Usual class full or cancelled | Unexpected free window | G1, G2, G4 |
| JTBD: second circumstance | One circumstance | Added "a friend invites me to try a new format" | G5 |
| JTBD: emerging third circumstance | Two circumstances | Logged "a specific training need my gym can't meet" (Hyrox prep) as an emerging circumstance, n=1 | G8 |
| Format focus | Reformer Pilates only | Hyrox/functional training noted as a second format to test: the Egypt boom NOVA described has a real Casablanca case | G8, NOVA founder |
| Price in the test | Not fixed | Full studio price in both arms, to separate price from commitment | G1 |
| Riskiest assumption status | Working hypothesis → contested | **Supported at reservation level** (A/B test); attendance still untested | A/B test |
| Owner value metric | New bookings | **Newcomers who come back**; one-off visitors have little value to owners | Owner D (and C, E wanting new-vs-regular tracking); Owner F expects newcomers to bring friends |
| Price as a rival | Logged, unresolved | Strengthened: a real goer says full price stops her (G6), matching 10 of 27 A/B declines | G6, A/B test |
| Phase 2 pilot studios | Not identified | Owners D and E approached first (both open to a trial) | Owners D, E |
| Owner outreach | The offer alone would convince owners | Involve a respected instructor or studio insider in outreach; address "why will this one last?" up front | NOVA founder |
| Phase 2 acquisition | Not defined | Test friend-referral and influencer channels for goers | NOVA founder, G5 |
| Evidence base | Card trials counted as pre-test signals | Card trials and Example-G3 relabelled as illustrative examples, not evidence | Sprint feedback (12 Oct) |

## 4. Primary test results (two-arm reservation test)
*Ran 11–12 Oct 2026 through `booking-test/`. Full table: `ab-test-results.md`.*

| | Arm A (no commitment) | Arm B (3-class pack) |
|---|---|---|
| Link sent to | 25 | 25 |
| Reserved ("I'M RESERVING" message) | 16 (64%) | 7 (28%) |
| Did not reserve | 9 | 18 |
| Top decline reasons | Price 3 · prefer my gym 3 · time 2 · coach 1 | Having to commit / buy a pack 10 · price 2 · prefer my gym 2 · time 2 · coach 1 · certainty 1 |
| Voice notes received | 15 | 14 |

**Result against threshold:** +36 pp with one-sided Fisher p = 0.011 → **≥30 pp with p < 0.05: Supports** · n per arm: 25
**Decision:** **Continue** to the Phase 2 pilot with real studios, measuring attendance and return at full price.

## 5. What we still don't know
- Whether anyone **actually attends** and pays (the test stopped at reserving; attendance is tested in the Phase 2 pilot).
- Whether trial goers come back at full price, which is what owners say they value (Owner D).
- Whether "won't commit" is about three classes or about paying 900 MAD at once (Arm B asked for both).
- Whether owners will commit a real class on a real date (all five answers are stated intent), and whether they'd choose this over their own discovery packs.
- Whether G1, G2, G5, G7 and Owners A–D are in our target cities.
- Whether owners who already sell single classes online (Owner F) need us at all, or only need awareness.
