# Market Alternatives — what owners and goers use instead

**Purpose:** map what our users would choose instead of us, using the course's four categories, and decide what each one means for the product. Supporting output for `ERRC.md`.
**Researched:** 11 Oct 2026. **Rule used:** for each alternative, market evidence (what exists) is kept separate from target-user behaviour (what our users actually do). If behaviour is unknown, it is labelled a hypothesis or gap.

**Evidence labels:** OBSERVED (seen in a real episode), REPORTED (said in an interview), DESK (public source), HYPOTHESIS (not yet evidenced).

---

## Bound the decision
- **Market:** independent boutique fitness studios (yoga, pilates, reformer, boxing) in Casablanca (pop. 3.2M) and Rabat (0.5M). About 54 listed studios, likely an undercount (`Fermi_estimate.md`).
- **Price level:** a single boutique class costs **250–400 MAD**, while an average monthly gym membership in Casablanca is **about 314 MAD**. **One boutique class costs roughly a month of gym.**
- **Decision this map informs:** what to compete on, what to leave alone, and which test comes first.

## Strongest current alternative
**Main strategic baseline (used in `ERRC.md`): doing nothing on the owner side** — running the class near-empty or cutting it, after discounting failed. Chosen because owners are the paying customer and this is what all three real owners described doing today.

Secondary baseline on the booker side: the already-paid gym membership, plus giving up when a DM booking stalls.

**Evidence basis:** REPORTED and OBSERVED behaviour from 4 goer interviews and 5 owner interviews, plus the A/B reservation test (50 class-goers, real reservation messages). Small samples, directional only. *(Card trials removed: they were illustrative examples.)*

## Competitive factors and trade-offs

| Factor | What alternatives assume or provide | Important trade-off, contradiction or gap |
|---|---|---|
| Price per class | 250–400 MAD for a boutique class; gym ≈ 314 MAD a month (DESK) | One class ≈ a month of gym, so goers weigh it against what they already pay (G1, G5, REPORTED; 10 of 27 A/B declines cited price or the gym) |
| Upfront commitment | Studios sell 3–50-class packs and 3–12-month subscriptions; discovery packs still mean 3 classes (DESK) | Newcomers must prepay before knowing the studio; G1 left half a 10-pack unused (REPORTED). In the A/B test, a 3-class pack cut reservations from 64% to 28% (OBSERVED) |
| Booking certainty and speed | Booking by Instagram DM or WhatsApp, or inside each studio's own app (DESK, OBSERVED G2, G4) | A slow reply loses the booking (G2, G4) |
| Price integrity for the owner | Discounts are the usual lever to fill quiet classes | Regulars notice and expect the same deal (Owners A–E, REPORTED) |
| Owner control over who gets which seat | Discounts and directories apply to everyone; studios' own tools don't separate newcomers from regulars for a given seat | Owners B, C, D and E will only take part if they choose seats and regulars don't see the offer (REPORTED) |
| Knowing whether new people came | No alternative reports new vs. regular attendees for a filled slot (team judgement) | Owners C, D, E want it; none track it today (REPORTED) |
| Variety of studios | Gyms and single studios offer one venue; no multi-studio pass found in Morocco (DESK) | Not something we compete on |

**Conventions taken for granted:** quiet slots are filled with discounts; newcomers commit to a pack before trying; booking a boutique class means messaging and waiting.

## Opportunity stress test (four lenses)

| Lens | Finding | Implication for the prototype |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry** | Low barrier: studios already sell discovery packs and could add a "single off-peak class" button themselves | Our value can't be the offer alone; it must be **reach to new goers + instant confirmation + new-vs-regular tracking**, which a solo owner won't build (Owner C) |
| **Substitution** | For goers, one class ≈ a month of gym, so the gym wins on price | Test whether goers accept full studio price for one off-peak class, or whether the price itself, not commitment, is the barrier (Persona 1: "price is the fixable part") |
| **Dependencies** | Depends on owners releasing seats and on WhatsApp/Instagram as the booking channel | Manual WhatsApp flow first; keep owners in control of which seats go out |
| **Major shifts** | **A boutique-fitness boom is a tailwind.** NOVA's founder says Egypt's pilates and Hyrox boom made it the right moment to launch (`interview-nova-founder.md`); Le360 reports reformer studios spreading fast in Morocco. A regional aggregator entering Morocco would change the field, but there is no sign of it yet | Start where demand is growing (reformer pilates); track new studio openings in Casablanca and Rabat as a timing signal |

## Strategic bets to test

| Hypothesis | Method | Metric | Threshold | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Removing commitment risk beats normal terms** (priority test) | Two-arm reservation test (`experiment-card.md`, `booking-test/`) | Reservation-rate gap between arms (20 per arm) | ≥30 pp, p < 0.05 | Met: keep. Under 15 pp: decline reasons pick the pivot (`pivot-log.md`) |
| Certainty of a spot matters more than commitment | Both arms promise confirmation within minutes; decliners tick their reasons | Decliners ticking "not sure I'd get a spot" | PROPOSED: the most-ticked reason | If met: pivot to an instant-booking tool for studios' existing classes |
| Owners prefer our offer to their own discovery pack for off-peak slots | Next owner interviews: show both side by side | Owners who would use ours for off-peak | PROPOSED ≥3 of 5 | Missed: help owners promote their own discovery packs instead |
| Goers will reserve at full studio price | Reservation test shows 300 MAD per class in both arms | Reservations at full price; decliners ticking "price" or "prefer my gym" | PROPOSED ≥10 of 40 reserve across both arms (25%) | Missed, with price the top reason: price, not commitment, is the barrier; rethink with owners |
