## 1. Owner/instructor F — reformer Pilates studio

**Profile:** runs a reformer-only Pilates studio. Clients book any class, time and coach through the studio's own online platform, first come first served. **A manager sets prices and decides how classes are filled.** She spoke for the manager, so her "yes" is second-hand.

| # | Topic | What she said | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Emptiest classes | The earliest slots, 7, 8 and 9 am, are empty or "at one fifth of their capacity" | REPORTED |
| 2 | Cost | "Either way we have to pay for the trainer", so those classes don't cover their cost | REPORTED |
| 3 | Why they're empty (her view) | People "don't know that there are classes", or are "very fixed to just one" place to exercise. If they knew the variety of coaches, times and "vibes", more would come | STATED (her diagnosis) |
| 4 | What they tried | (1) Made early-morning classes **cheaper**, "so that we can at least break even". (2) Changed the ambience: livelier music to motivate people in the morning. Problem: people only see this if they go into the studio's own platform | REPORTED |
| 5 | 2 spots for new people only, normal price | "I think that would make a very big difference", because fitness spreads "mouth to mouth": one newcomer who likes it brings a friend, and that friend brings another | STATED INTENT |
| 6 | What would get in the way | "Nothing in particular", because "this class never gets full" | STATED |
| 7 | Pricing | The manager set prices at the start and keeps them standard: "a lot of competition in the market, so we cannot go either too high or too low" | REPORTED |
| 8 | Would they try it? | "The manager would be very, very happy, and all the coaches as well", since it would help the class at least break even | STATED INTENT (on the manager's behalf) |

### What it supports or weakens

| Finding | Supports / weakens |
|---|---|
| Early-morning classes at about 1/5 capacity, trainer paid anyway | **Supports** the pain point (now 5 owners naming a specific quiet slot: A, C, D, E, F) |
| Lowered early-morning prices to break even | **Weakens** "all owners reject discounting": the first owner who **uses** a lower off-peak price. Now **5 of 6** reject or have abandoned discounting |
| Sees **awareness** as the cause ("people don't know") | **Supports the timing/awareness rival** (`assumptions.md` 1.1): for her, the problem is reach, not newcomers' fear of commitment |
| 2 newcomer spots would "make a very big difference"; word of mouth | **Supports** the offer and the friend-referral path (JTBD Circumstance B, G5; NOVA founder) |
| "Nothing would get in the way"; no conditions on seat control or hiding it from regulars | **Different from Owners B–E**, who all set conditions. Possibly because her studio already sells single classes on its own platform, so newcomers paying normal price isn't new to her regulars |
| Not the decision-maker | Her yes is second-hand: **the manager is the person to ask for a pilot** |
