## 1. Purpose

To learn how a real gym-goer in Rabat decides whether to try a class when he has free time, what stops him from trying a new studio, and how he would react to a single class at the normal price with no pack.

## 2. Participant profile

Youssef is a young professional in Rabat whose work schedule changes from week to week. He finds classes on Instagram and books them by direct message. He has no current studio, pack or membership.

## 3. Summary of answers

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Last free hour and a class | A client meeting was cancelled and he saw a boxing studio's Instagram story. He sent a DM, got no reply for about 40 minutes, gave up and ate lunch at his desk. |
| Q2 | Pack or membership history | He had a gym membership two years ago and barely used it because his hours change every week. He hasn't bought a pack since. |
| Q3 | What stops him trying a new studio | Booking only through WhatsApp or Instagram means waiting for a reply, which doesn't work when his free time comes up suddenly. He also doesn't want to waste money on a one-off. |
| Q4 | One class, normal price, no pack | Yes, the no-pack part helps because he can't plan ahead. But instant confirmation matters more. |
| Close | Referral | He offered to connect us with a colleague who takes classes. |

## 4. Key findings

- **Main barrier: certainty of a spot.** He dropped out at the booking step, not over price.
- **Secondary barrier: commitment.** A past membership went unused, so he avoids prepaying.
- **Trigger:** an unexpected free window, which matches the JTBD trigger in `pivot-log.md` row 6.
- **Price was not raised** as a barrier on its own.
