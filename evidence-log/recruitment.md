# Recruitment (test access)

## Who we test with
Regular boutique fitness class attendees (yoga, pilates, spin, boxing, etc.) in Morocco — no strict pre-filter on peak vs. off-peak; usual time slot is noted during outreach, not required for recruitment.

## How many
5 tested (the threshold is 3 of 5). Recruit 7–8 to cover no-shows/non-responses. No decision on a partial sample.

## How we reach them
One contact from Morocco (part of our IE cohort) refers regular fitness class attendees from his own personal network via WhatsApp. Direct one-on-one outreach per contact, offering the non-monetary nudge (priority access or social accountability matching) and manually coordinating their booking. If the contact also knows anyone connected to a boutique studio (owner, staff, or instructor), that's pursued opportunistically as a secondary channel — not required for the core test.

## By when
Have all possible contacts by September 15
