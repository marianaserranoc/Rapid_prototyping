# Recruitment (test access)

## Who we test with
Regular boutique fitness class attendees (yoga, pilates, spin, boxing, etc.) in Morocco — no strict pre-filter on peak vs. off-peak; usual time slot is noted during outreach, not required for recruitment. Assigned to one of two arms (low-commitment framing or normal-commitment framing) at the point of outreach, split as evenly as recruitment allows.

## How many
10 tested total, 5 per arm (Arm A: low-commitment framing; Arm B: normal-commitment framing) — the threshold is a ≥40 percentage-point gap between arms. Recruit 14–16 to cover no-shows/non-responses across both arms. No decision on a partial sample per arm.

## How we reach them
One contact from Morocco (part of our IE cohort) refers regular fitness class attendees from his own personal network via WhatsApp. Direct one-on-one outreach per contact, presenting the same off-peak slot with equal visibility — Arm A framed as low-commitment (no package required, explicit no-obligation guarantee) and Arm B framed as normal commitment (standard booking/payment terms) — and manually coordinating their booking. Arm assignment is tracked per contact to keep the comparison clean. If the contact also knows anyone connected to a boutique studio (owner, staff, or instructor), that's pursued opportunistically as a secondary channel for Layer 1 (owner interviews) — not required for the core test.

Have all possible contacts by September 23
