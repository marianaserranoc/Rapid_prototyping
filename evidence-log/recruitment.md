# Recruitment (test access)

## Who we test with

**Goers (primary test)**
Regular boutique fitness class attendees (yoga, pilates, reformer, spin, boxing) living in Casablanca or Rabat.

| Include | Exclude |
|---|---|
| Attended at least one boutique or group fitness class in the last 3 months | Already holds a package or membership at the host studio |
| Lives or works in Casablanca or Rabat | Close friends or family of any team member |
| Has at least one free window a week during off-peak hours (self-reported) | Works in the fitness industry (owner, staff, instructor) |
| | Has already seen the other version of the booking page |

Usual class time is noted at outreach but is not a filter. Each person is assigned to Arm A (link `#a`, no commitment) or Arm B (link `#b`, 3-class pack), alternating in the order they are contacted, and the arm is logged before the link is sent.

**Owners (Layer 1)**
Owners or managers of independent boutique studios in Casablanca or Rabat running around 15–25 classes a week, who set the schedule themselves.

**Host studio — not needed this round**
The reservation test uses a fictional studio (Atelier Sable) and measures reservations, not attendance, so no real studio is involved. A host studio from the owner interviews is needed for the Phase 2 pilot, where attendance is measured. Owners B, C, D and E made seat control a condition, so each host decides which seats are released.

## How many

| Group | Target | Recruit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goers, Arm A | 20 (minimum 15) | 25 | Covers people excluded (e.g. saw both versions) or unreachable. **Reached: 25** |
| Goers, Arm B | 20 (minimum 15) | 25 | Same. **Reached: 25** |
| Owners, Layer 1 | 5–8 total | 1–3 more | **6 real owners interviewed** (A–F; D and E on 12 Oct, F on 13 Oct) |
| Host studios (Phase 2) | 2–3 | 2–3 | Owners D and E first: both open to a trial; E agreed to a WhatsApp follow-up |

A person counts once their link is sent: no reply counts as "did not reserve". If fewer than 15 people per arm receive a link, the result is reported as partial with the actual n, not extrapolated.

**Why 20 per arm:** with 5 per arm one person moves the result by 20 pp; with 20 per arm a 30 pp gap is unlikely to be chance (`experiment-card.md`, Threshold).

## How we reach them
- **Goers:** 40 people is more than one network can supply, so we use three channels: (1) our Moroccan IE classmate's personal network; (2) other Moroccan students at IE and their contacts in Casablanca and Rabat; (3) referrals from the first participants ("know someone who takes classes?"). Each person gets one WhatsApp message with a link: `https://marianaserranoc.github.io/Rapid_prototyping/booking-test/#a` or `https://marianaserranoc.github.io/Rapid_prototyping/booking-test/#b`. Reservations and "not for me" replies arrive by WhatsApp with a hidden code (S1 = A, S2 = B). The team debriefs each person straight after and asks for a 1-minute voice note.
- **Owners:** the same contact, plus referrals from the five owners already interviewed, plus studios found on Google Maps and Instagram in Casablanca and Rabat contacted directly.
- **Host studio (Phase 2):** asked directly by the team member who interviewed them in person.

**Bias risks we accept and record:**
- Goers come from a few personal networks, so they may share habits and income level. We record which channel each person came from.
- Referred people may say yes to please the referrer. We count reservations sent through the page, not stated intent, to reduce this; it remains a weaker signal than attendance.
- **Cross-contamination between arms:** people in the same network may compare links. All links go out within the same 24 hours, and anyone who saw both versions is excluded.
- **Assignment is not truly random or blind.** We log each person's arm before sending so it can't be adjusted afterwards.
