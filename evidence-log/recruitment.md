# Recruitment (test access)

## Who we test with

**Goers (primary test)**
Regular boutique fitness class attendees (yoga, pilates, reformer, spin, boxing) living in Casablanca or Rabat.

| Include | Exclude |
|---|---|
| Attended at least one boutique or group fitness class in the last 3 months | Already holds a package or membership at the host studio |
| Lives or works within reach of the host studio | Close friends or family of any team member |
| Has at least one free window a week during off-peak hours (self-reported) | Works in the fitness industry (owner, staff, instructor) |

Usual class time is noted at outreach but is not a filter. Each person is assigned to Arm A (no commitment) or Arm B (normal commitment), alternating in the order they reply, and the arm is logged before the message is sent.

**Owners (Layer 1)**
Owners or managers of independent boutique studios in Casablanca or Rabat running around 15–25 classes a week, who set the schedule themselves.

**Host studio**
One of the three owners already interviewed (`personas/selected_personas/gym_owners.md`) hosts the off-peak class used in the primary test, so attendance can be confirmed with the studio. Owner B made seat control a condition, so the host decides which seats are released.

## How many

| Group | Target | Recruit | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goers, Arm A | 5 | 7–8 | Covers no-replies and no-shows |
| Goers, Arm B | 5 | 7–8 | Same |
| Owners, Layer 1 | 5–8 total | 2–5 more | 3 real owners already interviewed |
| Host studio | 1 | 1 | From the owners already interviewed |

If fewer than 5 people per arm complete the test, the result is reported as partial with the actual n, not extrapolated.

## How we reach them
- **Goers:** our Moroccan IE classmate refers regular class attendees from his personal network through WhatsApp. Each person gets one direct message with the same off-peak class, studio and price. The only difference between arms is one sentence on commitment terms (see `experiment-card.md`). A team member confirms bookings instantly in both arms.
- **Owners:** the same contact, plus referrals from the three owners already interviewed, plus studios found on Google Maps and Instagram in Casablanca and Rabat contacted directly.
- **Host studio:** asked directly by the team member who interviewed them in person.

**Bias risks we accept and record:**
- All goers come from one person's network, so they may share habits and income level.
- Referred people may say yes to please the referrer. We count real bookings and attendance, not stated intent, to reduce this.
