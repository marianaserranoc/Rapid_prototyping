#Opportunity

##PAIN POINT: 
A studio's recurring weekly off-peak class runs under capacity, and because they have fixed costs like maybe rent and instructor,  it doesn't matter how many people show up, every empty seat is money the studio loses. Discounting doesn't fix this, because it changes who shows up to grab a deal, not when people are actually willing to attend.

##USER: 
The owner/manager of a small, independent boutique studio running around 15-25 classes a week with few instructors where they personally set the schedule and watch attendance numbers class-by-class.

##NEED:
Owners need a way to convert the underfilled off-peaked seats into a more predictable and consistent attendance. This needs to be done without resorting to discounting, since this doesn't transform the type of customers. 

##INSIGHT:
Demand for off-peak classes isn't actually absent, it's more of a timing mismatch, not really a pricing problem. Discounting targets the wrong client, instead of the real one (when people are actually willing to show up).

##HOW MIGHT WE: 
How might we help boutique studio owners convert underfilled off-peak seats into predictable, steady attendance without relying on discounting?

##ONE SENTENCE: 
The owner/manager of a small, independent boutique studio needs to convert underfilled off-peak seats into predictable, steady attendance without relying on discounting, because demand for off-peak classes isn't absent it's a timing mismatch, not a pricing problem.
