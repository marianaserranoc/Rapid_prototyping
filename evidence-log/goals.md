# Goals

## Product Goal (Long-Term Outcome)
- **Specific:** Deploy a low-commitment booking mechanism for independent boutique fitness studios in Morocco that lets studio-goers trial recurring empty off-peak seats without full membership or package commitment — reducing the regret-aversion barrier that keeps them from booking, without relying on discounting.
- **Measurable:** Increase off-peak seat utilization at partner studios via trial bookings that convert to regular attendance, while maintaining standard full-commitment rates (no discounting) and without reducing peak-hour attendance.
- **Achievable:** Start with a small number of pilot boutique studios (independent, 15–25 classes/week) offering a lightweight, manually-coordinated trial mechanism rather than a full booking-system replacement.
- **Relevant:** Directly addresses the fixed-cost pain point (rent, instructor pay) and the discounting-fails-to-fill-seats problem established in `opportunity.md`, while targeting the goer-side regret-aversion barrier and the owner-side adoption risk identified in `assumptions.md` (Areas 1 and 2).
- **Time-bound:** [Open gap — no rollout timeline established yet; needs a real deadline once pilot studios are identified]

## Sprint Goal (This Cycle)
- **Specific:** Learn whether removing commitment risk (no package required, explicit no-obligation guarantee) produces meaningfully higher off-peak booking-and-attendance than an identical, equally visible offer with normal commitment terms — testing the riskiest assumption that goer regret-aversion, not timing/awareness, is the real driver of off-peak avoidance (Area 1).
- **Measurable:** Booking-and-attendance rate gap between the low-commitment arm and the normal-commitment arm, per `experiment-card.md`'s Primary Test — threshold is a ≥40 percentage-point gap between arms.
- **Achievable:** A small-scale, manual two-arm Wizard of Oz test (10 proxy testers total, no software development), run within this sprint, plus lightweight owner interviews (Layer 1) run in parallel.
- **Relevant:** Directly isolates the regret-aversion hypothesis from the competing timing/awareness and absence-of-demand hypotheses before any engineering investment, and checks the harder adoption gate (owner willingness) in parallel.
