# Expert Interview — Aly Shourbagui, founder of NOVA (Egypt)

**Who:** Aly Shourbagui, creator of NOVA, a credit-based booking platform for fitness studios in Egypt, the closest comparable model to our venture.
**Evidence type:** REPORTED by an operator in an analogous market. Not one of our target users, and not Morocco. It tells us what worked for NOVA, not what will work for us.

**Why we spoke to him:** until now, NOVA appeared in our files only as "understood to operate, from a reference deck" (`opportunity.md`). This interview replaces that weak proxy with a first-hand account, and covers both of our open adoption questions: will owners trust us, and will goers sign up?

---

## What he told us

### Owner side: why studios said yes
| # | What he said | Type |
|---|---|---|

| O3 | It was **easy for studios to understand the win-win**: empty seats filled, new clients for the studio, more choice for goers | REPORTED |

| # | What he said | Type |

| # | What he said | Type |

## What it means for our venture

| Finding | Connects to | What it supports or challenges | What we change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Studios had seen similar ideas fail (O1) | `open-gaps.md` Gap 8 (learned resignation); `jtbd.md` owner Push | **Supports** the risk that owners are sceptical before they hear the details. Owners' scepticism is about whether a platform will last and deliver, not only about discounting | Owner pitch must address "why will this one be different?" up front |
| Trust came from an instructor co-founder and visible commitment (O2) | `assumptions.md` Area 2; `ERRC.md` Create move | **New factor:** credibility of the team inside the fitness world drives owner adoption, not only the offer itself | New candidate assumption (below); involve a respected instructor in owner outreach |
| Owners quickly saw the win-win (O3) | `ERRC.md` value hypothesis | **Supports** that the two-sided value is easy to explain when trust exists | Lead owner conversations with the win-win, not the mechanism |
| A subscription failed; credit packages worked (G1, G2) | `assumptions.md` 1.1 (riskiest); `experiment-card.md` Arm B | **Mixed.** Supports that a *recurring* commitment puts people off. But goers *did* accept prepaying a package, which challenges the idea that any prepayment is the barrier | Read our reservation test with this in mind: if Arm B (3-class pack) performs close to Arm A, it matches NOVA's experience that packs are acceptable and only subscriptions are not |
| Launch timing matched a pilates and Hyrox boom (M1) | `Fermi_estimate.md` ("Pilates is the growth segment"); `market-alternatives.md` stress test, "Major shifts" | **Supports** that a booking platform works best when boutique demand is growing. Morocco shows a similar signal for pilates: Le360 (Jan 2026) describes reformer studios "springing up like mushrooms". We have **no evidence yet on Hyrox in Morocco** | Keep reformer pilates as the first focus; check whether Hyrox or similar formats are growing in Casablanca and Rabat before widening |
| Influencers and word of mouth drove growth (G3) | `market-alternatives.md` (trust/quality factor); `pivot-log.md` "pivot to trust" branch; `recruitment.md` channel 3 | **Supports** the trust and social-proof explanation: people try a studio when someone they follow or know vouches for it | Add influencer and referral channels to the Phase 2 pilot; keep the referral channel in recruitment |

### New candidate assumption (for `assumptions.md`, Area 2)
- **Must be true:** Owners will trust a new booking platform only when its team has visible credibility in the fitness world (an instructor or studio insider) and shows long-term commitment.
  - **Evidence needed:** We would need to observe owners agreeing to a pilot more often when an instructor or studio insider is part of the outreach than when the team approaches alone, from the next 5–8 owner conversations in Casablanca and Rabat.

## Evidence boundaries
- **One person, one market.** Egypt's studio market and payment habits may differ from Morocco's.
- **Survivorship and self-report.** A founder describing his own success may stress what worked and leave out what didn't.
- **No figures.** No conversion rates, studio counts or growth numbers were shared, so none are used here.
- **Not our target user.** This counts as expert evidence for our owner-adoption and go-to-market questions, not as evidence from Moroccan owners or goers.
