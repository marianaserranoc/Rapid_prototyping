# Blue Ocean — where Daba stops competing

**Purpose:** pull the blue-ocean thinking into one page. The detail lives in `ERRC.md` (the four actions), `market-alternatives.md` (alternatives, factors, value curve) and `opportunity-v02.md`. This file answers the question the course keeps asking: *with AI, anyone can build a similar app, so how are you creating your own blue ocean?*
**Status:** a value innovation **hypothesis**, partly supported by evidence. Not a validated blue ocean.

---

## 1. The red ocean today
Every alternative competes on the same factor: **price**.

| Who | How they fight for the empty seat | Why it fails |
|---|---|---|
| Studio owners | Discounts or cheaper off-peak classes | Regulars notice and expect the same deal. A and C stopped; B, D and E won't try it |
| Studios | Discovery packs (Core Studio: 3 classes for 600 MAD) | Still a prepaid commitment; in our A/B test a pack cut reservations from 64% to 28% |
| Gyms | A cheap monthly membership | Wins on price, but offers no boutique classes (G1, G5, G7 stay out of habit) |
| Aggregators (ClassPass-style) | Credits across studios | Not present in Morocco. Owners fear brand damage and lower prices |
| Goers | Instagram DM to book | Slow replies lose bookings (G2, G4) |

## 2. The move: stop competing on price

| Action | What Daba does | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|
| **Eliminate** | Discounting. Always the studio's normal price | **Evidence-supported:** Owners A–E (5 of 5) reject discounting; 16 of 25 newcomers reserved at full price. Against: proxy F's studio discounts; proxy G6 says full price stops her |
| **Reduce** | First-visit commitment, from a pack or membership to one class | **Evidence-supported at reservation level:** A/B test +36 pp (p = 0.011); G1, G4, G5, G8 avoid packs |
| **Raise** | Booking certainty: confirmed within minutes | **Plausible:** G2 and G4 gave up after slow DMs; not tested |
| **Create** | Owner-chosen seats, **for newcomers only**, plus a new-vs-regular report | **Plausible:** 4 of 5 owners (B–E) set exactly these conditions; the mechanism is untested |

**Strategy canvas:** `market-alternatives.md`, "Current value curve". Daba scores **low on price on purpose** and high on low commitment, certainty, price integrity, owner control and knowing who's new. No alternative has that shape.

## 3. Non-customers we unlock
Blue-ocean thinking looks beyond today's customers, to three tiers of non-customers.

| Tier | Who | Real evidence | What Daba changes for them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Soon-to-be** (on the edge of the market) | Gym members who'd like boutique classes "from time to time" | G1, G5, G7 | One class at a time, as a complement to the gym, not a replacement |
| **2. Refusing** (thought about it, said no) | People turned away by packs, memberships or intro courses | G8 (box wanted 900 MAD a month), G5 ("five up front is a different story"); 10 of 18 Arm B decliners | Removes the commitment that made them refuse |
| **3. Unexplored** (never considered) | Newcomers studios can't reach. **Owner F's insight**: people "don't know the classes exist" | Owner F (proxy), Owners D and E (Instagram reaches few) | Reach to new people, which a solo owner can't build alone |

## 4. "Anyone can copy this with AI": what's hard to copy

The app **is** easy to copy. The booking page we tested took a day. So the moat can't be the code. It has to be things that take time, trust or data:

| Moat | Why it's hard to copy | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The rule set owners asked for** | Newcomers only, full price, and owner-chosen seats, all together. Each is simple; together they answer every condition owners set | Owners B, C, D, E stated these conditions | Plausible |
| **Owner trust** | NOVA's lesson: studios had seen similar ideas fail and trusted NOVA because a co-founder was an instructor and the team stayed committed. Trust builds one studio at a time | NOVA founder interview | Plausible (expert, Egypt) |
| **Newcomer-vs-regular data** | Every pilot week adds data on which newcomers return. A copycat starts with zero; owners value exactly this (C, D, E) | Owners C, D, E want it; nobody tracks it today | Not yet built |
| **WhatsApp-first, in Darija and French** | Goers book by DM (G2, G4) and owners run on WhatsApp. Daba means "now" in Darija: book now, no pack | G2, G4; owner interviews | Decided (`specs/tech-stack.md`) |
| **First mover in Morocco** | No ClassPass-style platform found in Morocco | Desk research (`market-alternatives.md`) | Window, not a moat: it closes if someone else arrives |

## 5. What could turn this back into a red ocean
- **Studios copy the offer themselves.** Core Studio could sell a single class next to its discovery pack. Then Daba's value is only reach and tracking. **Test:** ask pilot owners whether they'd pay for those two things alone.
- **Price becomes the battleground anyway.** If newcomers don't come back at full price (assumption 1.2, now the riskiest), owners may start discounting through Daba, and we become a discounter in disguise.
- **An aggregator enters Morocco.** Credits across studios would compete for the same newcomers. Our answer would be owner control and no discounting, which aggregators don't offer.

## 6. Next evidence that would make it a real blue ocean
1. Owners D and E release real seats on a real date (adoption, not "I would").
2. Newcomers who reserve actually attend and pay full price (Phase 2).
3. At least 30% of them book again at full price (Phase 4). Without that, owners have no reason to stay.
