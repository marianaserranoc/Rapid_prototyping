# Persona hypothesis: The Regret-Averse Sampler

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

When an unplanned gap opens in her schedule and she notices an off-peak class at a studio she hasn't been to before, the closing window before the class starts prompts a young professional sampling boutique fitness in Casablanca to seek a way to use that free time on something she'll actually enjoy, without risking money or time on the wrong studio or a booking process that falls through.

## Behavioural archetype

A young professional in Casablanca with an unpredictable, last-minute schedule, who likes boutique fitness classes but hasn't committed to a studio or membership, and who abandons spontaneous bookings when uncertainty about an unfamiliar studio combines with booking friction against a closing time window.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Unpredictable, last-minute schedule; hasn't committed to a studio/package because she isn't confident she'll use it — "I don't always know when I'll be free. I usually decide quite last-minute... don't want to pay for a package when I'm not sure I'll actually use it."
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** When a gap suddenly opens, she considers an unfamiliar studio's off-peak class advertised on Instagram, DMs to book, and abandons if the studio doesn't reply quickly enough before the class starts — "I sent them a DM to ask about booking, but they didn't respond quickly. After waiting, I just gave up and went for coffee instead."
- **Current approach or alternative:** Discovery via Instagram, booking attempts via DM/WhatsApp, friends to de-risk unfamiliar studios, occasional multi-studio apps; falls back to free/flexible substitutes (coffee, home workout) when the attempt stalls.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Not price sensitivity — commitment aversion tied to uncertainty about fit/enjoyment: "My biggest constraint isn't necessarily the price itself. It's more that I don't want to commit to something when I'm not sure I'll use it or enjoy it."
- **Product or research decision affected:** Nova should not build a discovery-plus-discount platform. It needs a low-commitment, instant, spontaneous-booking mechanism, explicitly distinct from a discount — "I would want Nova to make trying a new studio... feel almost risk-free... I wouldn't want another platform that just gives me discounts."
