# Persona hypothesis: The Admin-Stretched Operator

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis · grounded in 1 real owner interview (O1) · requires real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

Owner-side JTBD in `jtbd.md`, carried forward as context. **Alignment flag:** the JTBD classifies operational/bandwidth load as adoption friction under Habit. For this Persona it is a primary constraint on which solutions are acceptable at all.

## Behavioural archetype

An owner-manager who runs bookings, changes, cancellations and payments largely by hand over WhatsApp, spends several hours a week on it with her staff, and manages quiet slots by watching attendance for months before cutting them — which suggests she may resist any off-peak mechanism that adds manual work (unvalidated — inferred, not asked directly), and she decides a slot's future on slow, informal evidence.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** ~20 classes/week, 4 instructors; a booking system for regulars, but new clients and many changes arrive through Instagram and WhatsApp.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** ~5–6 hours/week (owner + staff) answering WhatsApp messages, changing bookings, handling cancellations and payments; cut one morning class after watching low attendance "for a few months".
- **Current approach or alternative:** Manual WhatsApp handling plus a booking system; for chronically quiet slots, observe then cut.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Would reduce manual WhatsApp communication first if busier; balances instructor availability, room capacity and peak/off-peak demand herself; the value of an extra attendee depends on class and instructor cost.
- **Product or research decision affected:**
  1. **No added manual work.** Refunds, confirmations and trial bookings must run without the owner handling them over WhatsApp — otherwise this Persona won't adopt, whatever the goer-side result.
  2. **Slot-level evidence before cutting.** A useful owner-facing feature is showing whether a quiet slot is recoverable (trial bookings, return visits) before she spends months deciding to cut it.
  3. **Wizard of Oz cost.** The manual coordination in the current test is exactly the workload this Persona wants to shed — record the admin time per booking during the test as a feasibility signal.
