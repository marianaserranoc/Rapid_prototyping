## Persona 1: The Package-Wary Tryer


1. How do you currently stay active — gym, classes, home workouts?
2. How often do you go?
3. Have you tried boutique studio classes before — occasionally, or is it a habit?
4. Tell me about the last time you found a new studio — how did that happen, and what did you do next?
5. Did you end up booking it? What happened?
6. What made you not book it, specifically? Was it more that you weren't sure it'd be worth it, or that it cost more than what you already pay for? *(ask as two separate ideas)*

## Persona 2: The Uncommitted Last-Minute Booker

1. Do you have a regular studio, gym, or package right now, or does it vary?
2. Tell me about the last time you suddenly had free time and thought about a class.
3. What did you do next?
4. What happened after that — did you book it, or not?
5. If you didn't book it, walk me through exactly where it fell apart.
6. Was it more that you weren't sure it'd be worth it, or that the process of booking itself got in the way? *(ask separately)*
7. If the studio had confirmed your booking instantly, would that have changed what happened?
8. If instead they'd offered a full refund if you didn't like the class, would that have changed what happened?
9. Between those two — instant confirmation vs. a refund guarantee — which matters more to you, and why?
10. How often does this kind of situation happen for you?

## Persona 3: The Unanchored Sampler


1. Do you have a regular studio or package right now, or do you move between places?
2. Walk me through the last time you had a free window and thought about a class.
3. What happened next?
4. Was it more that you weren't sure it'd be worth it, or that it cost more than expected? *(ask as two separate ideas — this is the question the price-vs-distrust claim is cited to)*
5. If the price had matched what you expected, would you have booked?
6. **Live walk-through:** show me, on your phone, how you'd book a class for a free window tomorrow. *(observe where they stop — don't ask hypothetically. This is the question the "corroborated by walk-through" claim is cited to.)*
7. What's the least amount of info or signup you'd want before trying an unfamiliar class?
