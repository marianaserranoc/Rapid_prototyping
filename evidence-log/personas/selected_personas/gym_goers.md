## Persona 1: The Package-Wary Tryer

**Grounding status:** Grounded in two real interviews (G1, G5). Supported at group level by the A/B reservation test.

### Behavioural archetype
A person who already pays for a default way to train (a gym membership) and is curious about boutique classes, but will not prepay a pack before knowing a studio. She weighs one class against the gym she has already paid for. The moment to try comes either from an unexpected free window (G1) or from a friend's invitation (G5).

### Evidence boundary

| Claim | Evidence status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Has a gym membership and uses it as the default | REPORTED | G1 Q1–Q3; G5 Q2 |
| Found a studio through a friend (Instagram post, or repeated invitations) | REPORTED | G1 Q4; G5 Q1 |
| Free morning matched a class; didn't book, went to the gym | REPORTED | G1 Q4–Q5 |
| Keeps postponing a friend's invitation to reformer ("maybe next weekend") | REPORTED | G5 Q1 |
| Won't buy a pack before knowing the place | REPORTED | G1 Q6; G5 Q3 ("five up front is a different story"); G8 Q3 ("the commitment, honestly") |
| Skipped a class because the venue required a membership; did something else | REPORTED episode | G8 Q1 (box required 900 MAD a month; went running) |
| Stays with the gym out of habit even while wanting variety (machines, commute, friends, already paid) | REPORTED | G7 exchanges 6 and 9 |
| Past or secondhand experience of unused packs drives this | REPORTED | G1 Q9 (used 5–6 of a 10-pack); G5 Q2 (friends' unused sessions: secondhand) |
| One class felt expensive next to the gym | REPORTED | G1 Q6; G5 Q1 ("prices felt high") |
| Full price for one class is acceptable; no discount needed | STATED INTENT | G5 Q4 |
| Nervous about being the only beginner; Instagram doesn't show the class | REPORTED | G5 Q3 |
| Instructor is the main reason to return | REPORTED | G1 Q10 |
| Removing the pack makes this kind of person more likely to reserve | **OBSERVED, group level** | A/B test: 16/25 vs. 7/25 at the same price per class; "having to commit" was the top reason for not reserving (10 of 27). Not linked to individual personas |
| Price is a separate barrier from commitment | **OPEN** | "Price" and "prefer my gym" were 10 of 27 declines in the A/B test; G1 and G5 both mention price |
| Holds for people without a gym membership | UNKNOWN | All four interviewees are gym members |

**What changed after feedback:** the earlier version said "price, not trust, was the primary blocker" based on a card trial. That trial was an example, so the claim is removed. Real evidence now says **commitment is a barrier (G1, G5, A/B test) and price is a live second one**, not that price dominates.

## Persona 2: The Uncommitted Last-Minute Booker

**Grounding status:** Grounded in two real interviews with the same recalled episode (G2, G4).

### Behavioural archetype
A young professional whose schedule changes week to week. Free time appears without warning (a cancelled meeting), he finds a class on Instagram and tries to book by DM. If nobody confirms quickly, the window closes and he gives up. He also avoids prepaying because a past membership went unused.

### Evidence boundary

| Claim | Evidence status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Unpredictable schedule; free time appears at short notice | REPORTED | G2 Q2, Q10; G4 Q1–Q2 |
| Discovers on Instagram, books by DM or WhatsApp | REPORTED | G2 Q2–Q3; G4 Q1, Q3 |
| Cancelled meeting → Instagram story → DM → no quick reply → gave up | REPORTED episode | G2 Q2–Q5; G4 Q1 (no reply for ~40 min, ate lunch at his desk) |
| Slow confirmation caused the drop-out | SUPPORTED by the episodes themselves | G2 Q5–Q6; G4 Q3 |
| Avoids prepaying after an unused membership | REPORTED | G4 Q2 |
| Main stated worry is wasting money, not the price itself | REPORTED | G2 Q6; G4 Q3 |
| Instant confirmation matters more than removing the pack | STATED | G4 Q4 |
| Would still book a no-pack class if confirmation were instant | **UNTESTED for this persona** | The A/B test held confirmation constant in both arms, so it can't rank certainty against commitment |

**What changed after feedback:** the earlier version "closed the gap" with a card trial (4 of 5 vs. 2 of 5). That trial was an example, so it is removed. The certainty finding now rests on **two real people who did the same thing** (G2, G4). That is still n=2, and the episodes are very similar, so the team will check G4's recording against the report.

## Persona 3: The Unanchored Sampler

**Grounding status: HYPOTHESIS (reverted 12 Oct), with a first real anchor (13 Oct).** The earlier version said "Grounded — interview Example-G3, corroborated by WoZ trial". Both sources were illustrative examples, not real people, so the persona went back to being a hypothesis. On 13 Oct, G6 (interviewed by Mariana) gave it one real anchor: she wants variety across studios and finds single classes too expensive. One person is not enough to call it grounded.

### Behavioural archetype (hypothesis)
A studio-goer with flexible time and no fixed studio, who moves between studios and hesitates at the pack-vs-drop-in choice at the booking step.

### Evidence boundary

| Claim | Evidence status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Wants to move between studios rather than be tied to one | **REPORTED, n=1** | G6 (13 Oct): a membership kept her at one studio "while new places were opening up" that she couldn't try |
| Hesitates at the price of a single class | **REPORTED, n=1** | G6: one class is "very expensive"; she'd try it only if cheaper as a trial. **This works against our full-price offer** for this persona |
| Accepts a phone number and one-tap confirmation | HYPOTHESIS | No real source |
| Would return after a trial | UNKNOWN | Not tested anywhere; moves to the Phase 2 pilot |

**Why we keep it:** if multi-studio samplers exist in Casablanca and Rabat, they're the people most likely to use a newcomers-only offer repeatedly across studios, which is a different business from Personas 1 and 2. **To ground it:** one or two interviews with people who use several studios. Ask the A/B participants who reserved whether they already rotate between studios.

## What the real evidence says across personas

- **Commitment is a real barrier.** Three of four real goers avoid packs (G1, G4, G5), and in the A/B test removing the pack more than doubled reservations at the same price per class.
- **Certainty matters for last-minute bookers.** Two of four real goers lost a booking to a slow DM reply (G2, G4). The product keeps instant confirmation for this reason, even though the A/B test didn't measure it.
- **Price is not settled.** It came up in two interviews (G1, G5) and in 10 of 27 A/B declines.
- **There are two ways in:** an unexpected free window (G1, G2, G4) or a friend's invitation (G5). See `JTBD.md`.
