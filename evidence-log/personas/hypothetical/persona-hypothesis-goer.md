# Persona hypothesis: The Group-Chat Explorer

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

When a studio-goer with an existing fitness routine has their usual class slot become unavailable, the resulting forced binary go/no-go decision on a visible-but-unproven off-peak option prompts them to seek a way to protect their workout week without exposing themselves to unclear commitment risk or unresolved quality doubt about something they've never tried.

## Behavioural archetype

A fitness-interested person who discovers new studios and sports through a booking platform, initiates or joins group tries via friend chats, and treats variety across studios and modalities as the point — currently doing so under a free trial that removes all financial risk.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Active social life, fitness is part of lifestyle, has a friend group that shares and coordinates fitness activities via group chat. Currently using ClassPass on a free trial month.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Browses ClassPass, finds a new studio or sport, sends the link to the friend group chat. If schedule fits and friends are in, they go together. Sometimes goes alone but prefers the social experience. Willing to travel for novelty but drops options that are too far.
- **Current approach or alternative:** ClassPass free trial as the discovery and booking mechanism. Friend-initiated group chat coordination as the decision-making process. No commitment to any single studio — the platform handles all access.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:**
  - Schedule fit is a hard filter — if it doesn't fit, the option is dead
  - Distance has a ceiling — novelty raises tolerance but "too far" kills it
  - Social initiation is a strong preference, not a hard constraint — she'll go alone, but gravitates toward group experiences
  - Currently operating under zero financial risk (free trial) — behaviour under real cost is unknown
- **Product or research decision affected:** The current two-arm Wizard of Oz experiment tests commitment risk as a solo individual decision. This Persona doesn't overturn that focus, but flags that the social-initiation dynamic (platform discovery → group-chat sharing → going together) is a second variable the test is blind to. If this Persona is real and common, the mechanism may need a social layer to reach this person — worth testing in a follow-up round, not at the cost of the current primary test.

## Validation gaps

1. **Free-trial dependency (highest priority):** All observed behaviour occurred during a zero-cost trial month. Whether variety-seeking and weekly exploration persist when real money is involved is completely unknown — if it stops, this Persona may not exist as a durable pattern.
2. **Platform dependency:** This person discovers everything through ClassPass. It's unknown whether she would encounter or engage with a mechanism offered by an individual studio for its own off-peak slots, since her current workflow bypasses studio-level marketing entirely.
3. **Regret-aversion relevance:** The riskiest assumption in the venture is that goer regret-aversion blocks booking. This Persona hasn't shown any regret-aversion behaviour — all risk was pre-neutralised by the free trial and social group. Whether she would experience regret-aversion under normal conditions is untested.
