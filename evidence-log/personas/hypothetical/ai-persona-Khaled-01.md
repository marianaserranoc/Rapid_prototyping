# Persona hypothesis: The Package-Wary Tryer

**Status:** Selected critical Persona · grounded in 1 real goer interview (G1) · requires further real-user validation

Goer-side JTBD in `jtbd.md`, carried forward as context. **Alignment flag:** G1's trigger was an unplanned free window after seeing a friend's post, not "usual slot becomes unavailable" — see Validation gaps.

## Behavioural archetype

A person who already pays for a default way to train (a gym membership) and occasionally tries boutique classes when a free window appears, but will not prepay a package before knowing a studio — and who weighs a single boutique class against the default they have already paid for, so when unsure they fall back to that default.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Already has a paid, habitual workout option; boutique classes are occasional extras, often found through friends on Instagram.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Found a studio via a friend's post, asked price and schedule on WhatsApp, had a free Friday morning that fit, did not book, went to the usual gym instead.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Pays per class rather than buying packages; falls back to the already-paid gym.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Past package loss (used 5–6 of 10 classes) makes them avoid prepaying; single-class price is judged against the near-zero marginal cost of the gym; price often isn't visible without messaging.
- **Product or research decision affected:**
  1. The low-commitment offer has to beat a **paid fallback**, not just "nothing" — so the Wizard of Oz arms should record whether each tester has a gym/membership, and Arm A's message should address quality proof (instructor, what the class is like) as well as refundability.
  2. The offer must show the price up front, since having to message for a price is itself a step where this Persona drops out.
  3. Return decision is driven by the experience (instructor first) — the success metric for this Persona is the *second* visit, not the first booking.
