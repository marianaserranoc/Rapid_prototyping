# Persona hypothesis: The Unprompted Free-Window

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis · grounded in 1 reported episode from goer interview G1 · requires real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

Goer-side JTBD in `jtbd.md`, carried forward as context. **Alignment flag:** this Persona never reaches a go/no-go decision on a visible off-peak option — the option is invisible at the moment free time appears.

## Behavioural archetype

A person who sometimes gets unplanned free time during the day and would train in it, but does not search for classes in that moment and does not know what is running nearby — so the free window passes to rest by default. The barrier is not reached at the commitment step; it happens before any option is seen.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Irregular daytime free windows (e.g. finishing work early) that are not planned in advance.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Finished work early with 2–3 free hours, did not work out, went home, ate, watched something.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Rest / nothing. Would check Instagram or Google only if already intending to train.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Doesn't know which classes run at that time; "wouldn't necessarily think" to look; Instagram doesn't show real availability.
- **Product or research decision affected:**
  1. This Persona is the living version of the **timing/awareness competing hypothesis**. If it is common, a refund guarantee does nothing for them — they need the off-peak option shown to them at the moment of free time.
  2. The Wizard of Oz test gives both arms equal visibility, so it **cannot** measure this Persona's barrier. The team should record in `experiment-card.md` that a positive result for regret-aversion does not rule out awareness as a second, separate barrier.
  3. Any product with a "push available off-peak slots" feature should be tested against this Persona, not the Package-Wary Tryer.
