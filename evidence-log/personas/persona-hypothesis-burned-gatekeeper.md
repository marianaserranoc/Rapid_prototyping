# Persona hypothesis: The Burned Gatekeeper

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

When an independent boutique studio owner personally watches a recurring off-peak class run underfilled, class after class, the resulting real-time frustration — compounded by a past failed attempt at discounting — prompts her to seek a way to reliably fill that slot on terms she still controls, without retraining her clients to expect discounted pricing or requiring her to hand pricing and scheduling decisions to a system she doesn't fully trust.

## Behavioural archetype

An independent boutique studio owner hypothesized to personally monitor attendance, have a negative experience with discounting, absorb recurring off-peak losses, and value control over pricing and scheduling.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Hypothesized sole or near-sole operator of a small independent boutique studio (15–25 classes/week, few instructors). Believed to personally set the schedule and watch attendance class-by-class. Hypothesized to have tried discounting in the past with negative results (attracting deal-hunters, cannibalizing full-price regulars). Assumed to have limited admin bandwidth.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Hypothesized to passively absorb the financial loss of underfilled off-peak slots, with no systematic or repeatable approach to filling them. Sporadic, low-effort gestures (asking regulars to bring friends, social media posts) are a team hypothesis — more specific than the evidence currently supports.
- **Current approach or alternative:** Hypothesized passive acceptance of empty seats as the default. Discounting is hypothesized as a past failed attempt, not a current practice. No active, consistent alternative has been confirmed.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:**
  - Hypothesized to be burned on discounting — anything perceived as a discount is expected to be rejected, but no owner has confirmed this experience
  - Hypothesized control attachment — believed to be resistant to delegating pricing/scheduling decisions to an outside platform, but potentially overstated from a single circumstantial detail ("personally sets the schedule")
  - Hypothesized low admin bandwidth — any mechanism requiring significant operational overhead is expected to be a non-starter, but unverified
  - All three constraints are HYPOTHESIS — no owner interview has been conducted
- **Product or research decision affected:** If this Persona is real, the team would need to lead with control and proof, not convenience. Layer 1 interviews and the eventual owner-facing pitch would need to demonstrate first that the mechanism is genuinely different from discounting, requires almost no extra work, and lets her retain control. Recruitment for owner interviews may need direct, trust-based outreach rather than relying only on WhatsApp referrals through a single contact.

## Validation gaps

1. **Control attachment (highest priority):** If owners would happily delegate pricing/booking to a platform that works, the core behavioural distinction of this Persona collapses — she'd be a pragmatic adopter, not a burned gatekeeper.
2. **Discounting scar:** The entire "burned on discounting" claim is team interpretation, not sourced from any real owner. If owners haven't actually tried discounting, or tried it and it worked fine, the resistance assumption disappears.
3. **Passive absorption as default:** It's possible owners have systematic responses to off-peak underfill that the team simply hasn't encountered — cutting the class, restructuring the schedule, running private sessions in the slot. If so, the "absorber" framing is wrong.
4. **Everything is HYPOTHESIS:** No single claim in this Persona has been validated with a real owner. Layer 1 interviews are the minimum viable next step before treating any of this as grounded.
