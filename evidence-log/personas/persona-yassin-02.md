# Persona hypothesis: The Protective Curator

**Status:** Provisional Persona hypothesis requiring real-user validation

## Shared Job to Be Done

When a recurring class is under-booked shortly before it starts, and fixed costs (instructor, rent) will be incurred regardless, checking the roster and seeing it under-filled prompts an independent boutique studio owner in Casablanca to seek a way to capture incremental revenue from the empty seat without cheapening the class in the eyes of her existing loyal customers or losing control of her pricing and schedule.

## Behavioural archetype

A hands-on boutique studio owner in Casablanca who personally controls scheduling and attendance, runs under-filled classes anyway rather than disappoint a regular, and is open to third-party demand only on the condition that it doesn't read as a discount or disrupt her existing customer relationships.

## Decision-relevant profile

- **Relevant context:** Very involved in day-to-day running of her studio; controls the schedule, watches attendance, protects the studio's image and pricing — "I'm very involved in the day-to-day running of my studio. I control the schedule, keep an eye on attendance, and care a lot about maintaining the studio's image and pricing." She notes she hasn't spoken with Nova directly, so part of her account is expectation rather than lived experience.
- **Most relevant current behaviour:** Faced with a near-empty class, she runs it anyway for a regular's sake rather than cancel, and would try Instagram/WhatsApp/referrals before considering a third-party platform — "I had an off-peak class with only two people booked... I decided to run it because one of the people was a regular and I didn't want to disappoint her."
- **Current approach or alternative:** Instagram stories, WhatsApp broadcasts, DMs, referrals, occasional promotions; some bookings and payments still handled manually.
- **Decision-relevant constraints:** Revenue, control, and trust — specifically, protecting existing-customer relationships and pricing, and avoiding added administrative work — "My biggest concerns are revenue and control, but also trust. I need to know that a platform won't damage my relationship with existing customers or create a lot of extra work for me."
- **Product or research decision affected:** Nova should not approach studios as a discount marketplace. It needs owner-controlled seat allocation, no visible discounting, and minimal admin overhead — "I would want Nova to help me monetize seats that would otherwise stay empty, while allowing me to keep my normal prices for regular customers... I should be able to decide which classes and how many seats I want to make available."
