# Opportunity Frame — Nova

## Business idea
Nova is a "ClassPass-style" app for Morocco: one place to discover and book fitness classes across many independent gyms and studios (boxing, yoga, pilates, spin, crossfit, etc.), instead of booking with each studio separately.

## Pain point
Young professionals who are starting a fitness habit want to try different studios and class types, but booking is scattered across each studio's own app, Instagram DMs, or WhatsApp. The friction of juggling multiple booking channels pushes them back to whichever single gym they already have a membership at — even when it isn't the class or studio they actually wanted.

**Illustrative story (placeholder — not yet real evidence, see Open gaps):**
Yasmine, 27, a marketing associate in Casablanca, decided in March to start working out regularly. A coworker recommended a boxing studio in Maarif, she'd seen a yoga studio on Instagram, and a spin class opened near her office in Twin Center. Booking the boxing class meant downloading a studio-specific app just for that one gym. The yoga studio only took bookings via Instagram DM, and she waited most of a day for a reply with available times. The spin studio required a phone call during business hours, which she kept missing. After a week of chasing three different booking processes, she gave up trying anything new and renewed her old, familiar gym membership instead — even though it wasn't the class experience she actually wanted.

## USER
Young professionals in Casablanca (roughly 24–35) who are new to, or just restarting, a structured fitness habit, and who want to explore different class types and studios rather than commit to one gym long-term.

## NEED
To discover and book fitness classes across multiple studios, instantly, from one place — without downloading a separate app or messaging each studio individually.

## INSIGHT
The friction of juggling separate apps, DMs, and phone calls outweighs the appeal of trying something new. So people default back to the one gym they already know — not because it's what they want, but because it's the path of least resistance. The switching cost, not the price or interest, is what kills variety-seeking.

## HOW MIGHT WE
How might we let someone browse and book any class, at any partner studio, from one app — in less time than it takes to send an Instagram DM?

## Point of View (combined sentence)
**Young professionals starting a fitness habit** need **to discover and book classes across multiple studios instantly, from one place** because **the friction of juggling separate booking channels pushes them back to the one gym they already know, even when it isn't what they actually want.**

## Open gaps
- The story above is an **illustrative placeholder** I generated to show the shape of the problem — it is not a real user account yet. Replace it with an actual conversation before treating this as evidence (see `recruitment.md`).
- "Nothing like this exists in Morocco yet" is an unconfirmed belief, not a market scan — needs a quick competitor check (existing apps, gym aggregators, or informal Facebook/WhatsApp groups doing this already, in Casablanca and other major cities).
- Frequency/willingness-to-pay ("multiple times a week, high stakes") is stated as an assumption, not evidence from a real user yet.
- Whether "young professionals" specifically (vs. students or serious athletes) is the right first wedge is still unvalidated — this was chosen to sharpen focus, not confirmed by data.
- Casablanca was chosen as the starting city because it's Morocco's largest and most commercially active city — this hasn't been validated against other cities (Rabat, Marrakech, Tangier) either.
