# Goals — Nova

## Product Goal
Enable **young professionals starting a fitness habit in Casablanca** to **discover and book any class at any partner studio from one app** so that **they can build a consistent fitness habit without the friction of juggling separate booking channels for every studio.**

*This is an intended outcome, not a fixed feature list or solution — it stays the team's shared direction across several build/test cycles and only changes if evidence materially changes the opportunity.*

## Sprint Goal (current)
**Learn whether enough boutique studios in Casablanca will agree to open calendar access and accept a revenue split with Nova, strongly enough to justify building the studio-facing side of the product.**

This targets the current riskiest assumption from `assumptions.md` (Area 2: Supply). It is a learning outcome, not a task — the "smallest useful test" (e.g. structured conversations with studio owners, captured in `experiment-card.md`) is chosen to serve this goal, not the other way around.

## Open gaps
- No studios have confirmed yet — this Sprint Goal will be tested via direct outreach (see `recruitment.md` for who to approach and `experiment-card.md` for how the test is designed).
- The Sprint Goal will likely change once Area 2 evidence comes in: if studios say yes easily, the next riskiest assumption (demand or pricing) becomes the new focus.
- Casablanca was chosen as the starting city by default (largest, most commercial) — not yet validated against other Moroccan cities.
