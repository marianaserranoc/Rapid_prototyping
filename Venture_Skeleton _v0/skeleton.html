This dashboard traces the full venture logic from opportunity to decision rule — what's confirmed, what's still an open gap, and what one small test is designed to settle.

Opportunity Frame

The problem, the person who feels it, and why it exists — with what's confirmed kept separate from what still needs evidence.

Pain Point
A studio's recurring weekly off-peak class runs under capacity. Because rent and instructor pay are fixed regardless of attendance, every empty seat is money lost outright. Discounting doesn't fix this — it attracts deal-hunters rather than net-new attendees.
Confirmed reasoning
Target User
Owner/manager of a small, independent boutique studio — 15–25 classes/week, sets their own schedule, watches attendance class-by-class.
Gap: modality & geography
Need
A way to convert underfilled off-peak seats into predictable attendance without discounting, since discounting changes who shows up for a deal, not when people are willing to attend.
Gap: restates the HMW
Insight
Demand for off-peak classes isn't absent — it may be a timing mismatch, not a pricing problem. Competing explanation, not yet ruled out: demand for that slot is simply absent.
Riskiest assumption
