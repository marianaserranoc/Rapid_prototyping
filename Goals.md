# Assumptions

## Area 1: Studio Supply & Partnership
- **Must be true:** Independent studio owners have recurring off-peak vacancies and are willing to list them on a multi-studio platform without fear of diluting their primary membership prices.
- **Evidence needed:** Direct agreement or intent-to-join signups from studio managers allocating a minimum number of weekly off-peak spots.

## Area 2: User Demand & Value Proposition
- **Must be true:** Fitness-seekers prefer paying for multi-studio flexibility over fixed single-gym subscriptions, specifically during off-peak hours.
- **Evidence needed:** Landing page conversion rates or pre-orders for flexible pass packages.

## Area 3: Operational & Check-in Feasibility
- **Must be true:** Studios will accept a lightweight check-in mechanism (e.g., manual verification or simple digital voucher) without requiring custom integration with their existing management software.
- **Evidence needed:** Studio owner acceptance of a manual or low-tech verification process during testing.

## Priority
- **Riskiest assumption:** Independent studio owners are willing to allocate unsold off-peak seats to a third-party multi-studio platform.
- **Why:** If studio owners refuse to list their excess capacity, there is no inventory to offer users, rendering the consumer side irrelevant.

## Open gaps
- The exact revenue-split percentage required for studio owners to agree to partnership.
